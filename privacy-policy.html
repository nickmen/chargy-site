Chargy! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Chargy! is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Chargy! has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars earned, settings and which story scenes you have seen) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the full game and restore it on your devices.

Children

Chargy! is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
