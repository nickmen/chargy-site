Chargy! Support

Need help with Chargy!, found a bug, or have an idea? Email parcelwish@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I play?

Move Chargy with the joystick on the left. Aim and tap the red hook button on the right to fire your cable, grab critters and pull them into the plugs. Clear a level with fewer hooks to earn more stars.

What's free, and what's the purchase?

The whole first room, the Bedroom (20 levels), is free. A one-time purchase, All Worlds, unlocks the Kitchen, Attic, Bathroom and Fuse Box: 80 more levels, 4 bosses and the ending. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought All Worlds but the game is locked again.

Open any locked world and tap Restore purchases on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Can my family use my purchase?

Yes. All Worlds supports Family Sharing.

I'm stuck on a level.

Read the hint at the top of the screen, and remember that every object in a level is there for a reason. You can restart any time with the button at the top right.

Will my progress carry over to a new device?

Progress is saved on your device only. Your purchase can always be restored on any device with the same Apple ID.
